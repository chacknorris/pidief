# Code of Conduct

PIDIEF is a small open-source project. The standard for participation is straightforward: be respectful, practical, and constructive.

## Expected behavior

- Be polite and assume good intent.
- Focus feedback on the work, not the person.
- Welcome contributors with different levels of experience.
- Share context when disagreeing so others can evaluate the tradeoff.
- Respect privacy and security concerns, especially around document handling.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or intimidation
- Bad-faith arguments or deliberate disruption
- Personal attacks in issues, pull requests, discussions, or other project spaces
- Publishing private information without permission
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention

## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate this code of conduct. Repeated or severe violations may lead to a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainers privately through the security contact flow in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
